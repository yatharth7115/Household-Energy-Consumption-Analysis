02 / Where it went

Four parts of the whole.

Three measured sub-metering channels and a calculated remainder account for the observed energy. The remainder is not a directly measured appliance group.

SHARE OF OBSERVED ENERGY37,283.7 kWh
SUB-METERING 012,299kWh · 6.2%
SUB-METERING 022,661kWh · 7.1%
SUB-METERING 0313,235kWh · 35.5%
CALCULATED REMAINDER19,088kWh · 51.2%

Each reading estimates total energy as active power × 1,000 ÷ 60 Wh. The remainder subtracts all three sub-metering readings. It is negative for 1,050 complete minutes; those calculated values are retained to preserve the total.

04 / Method & perspective

What the numbers mean.

The analysis is designed to be reproducible and honest about what a single household dataset can and cannot show.

01 / CLEAN

Start with complete readings

From 2,075,259 source rows, 2,049,280 have complete timestamps and measurements. Missing values are dropped rather than invented.

02 / AGGREGATE

Count observed energy

One-minute energy is summed into monthly kWh. The coverage strip makes partial months and missing minutes visible alongside the totals.

03 / COMPARE

Hold the sample constant

All three algorithms use the same seeded 5,000-row sample and six standardized features. PCA only makes the cluster map viewable.

04 / INTERPRET

Stay within the evidence

Clusters describe patterns, not named appliances or future demand. The five-group choice and DBSCAN radius are exploratory settings.
